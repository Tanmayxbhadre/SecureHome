End-to-End Encrypted

Every message is encrypted on your phone using ECDH P-256 and AES-256-GCM. The relay in the middle cannot decrypt anything it carries.

Zero Persistent Storage

Messages stay in relay memory for at most 15 minutes before being permanently purged. There is no central database or user accounts.

How to Install on Android

Simple 3-step setup to get SecureHome running in 60 seconds.

1

Download & Install APK

Tap Download APK above or scan the QR code. When Android asks, select Settings → Allow from this source to complete the installation.

2

Allow SMS Permission

Open SecureHome and tap Allow SMS access. The app reads incoming messages locally to spot OTPs. No messages are ever uploaded unencrypted.

3

Pair with Laptop or Phone

Open the Web Viewer on your laptop. On your phone, tap Devices → Pair a device and scan the screen. Done!

# Install via USB with ADB:
adb install -r securehome.apk
# Forward laptop port to phone over USB (allows http://localhost:8787 directly on phone):
adb reverse tcp:8787 tcp:8787
